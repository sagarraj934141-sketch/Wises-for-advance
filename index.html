The first secret

A little riddle to open the first door.

Locked

I can be felt but not held. I can grow without being seen. What am I?

“Pyaar sirf kisi ko paane ka naam nahi hota, pyaar toh us insaan ko samajhne ka naam hota hai jise tumne khud se bhi zyada chaha ho. Sabse zyada dard tab hota hai jab wahi insaan tumhari aankhon mein aansu dekhkar bhi tumhara dard samajh nahi paata, jiske liye tumne apni har khushi ko side mein rakh diya ho. Mohabbat mein insaan tab nahi toot-ta jab koi use chhod deta hai, balki tab toot-ta hai jab woh insaan saath toh hota hai, par pehle jaisa apna nahi lagta… 💔 Aur sach kahun, toh pyaar ki sabse khoobsurat aur dardnaak baat yahi hai—dil usi se shikayat karta hai, jiske bina rehne ka khayal bhi dil ko manzoor nahi hota.” 🥺❤️‍🩹
02

The hidden note

The next room appears when the first is open.

Locked

I travel from one heart to another, even when there are no words. What am I?

“Feeling woh ehsaas hai jo lafzon mein kabhi poora bayaan nahi ho sakta… jahan ek chhoti si baat dil ko khush kar deti hai aur ek chhoti si berukhi poori raat rula deti hai. Jab kisi se sach mein feeling ho jaati hai na, toh uska online aana bhi sukoon deta hai, uska ek message chehre par muskurahat la deta hai, aur uska badla hua lehja dil mein hazaar sawaal khade kar deta hai. Sabse zyada dard tab hota hai jab hum kisi ko apni har feeling samjhana chahte hain, lekin woh hamari khamoshi tak samajhna chhod deta hai… 💔 Aur pata hai, asli feeling kya hoti hai? Kisi se naraz hokar bhi usi ke message ka intezaar karna, usse door hokar bhi usi ke kareeb rehna chahna, aur andar se tootkar bhi uske saamne theek hone ka dikhawa karna. “Kyunki jahan sirf attraction hota hai, wahan insaan badal jaata hai… lekin jahan sachchi feeling hoti hai, wahan insaan badalne se pehle sau baar sochta hai ki kahin uske badalne se kisi apne ka dil toh nahi toot jaayega.” 🥺❤️‍🩹

Chapter 04 · No audience, just us

A letter that
doesn't need a reply.

To my Princess,

On your birthday, I could give you a thousand beautiful things. Flowers that fade, gifts that get put away, words that disappear into a busy day.

Instead, I wanted to make you a little place you could return to. A reminder that you matter, that your happiness matters, and that the person you are is worth celebrating in all the ways that count.

I hope the year ahead brings you quiet peace on difficult days, unexpected laughter on ordinary ones, courage when you need it, and people who remind you how wonderful you are.

And when life gets a little loud, I hope you remember this: you don't have to earn every beautiful thing by being strong all the time. You deserve kindness simply because you are you.

Happy birthday, Princess. May this next chapter be kinder, brighter, and full of moments that make you smile when you remember them.

Always wishing you the best, ♡
